Privacy Policy for No Cap Escape

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how No Cap Escape handles information when you play our mobile game on Android.

1. Local Game Data (On This Device)

No Cap Escape does not require an account, login, or registration. Your game progress—including current level, Aura balance, Block Skins, Aura Room upgrades, daily streak, and sound settings—is stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

No Cap Escape displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

Where required by regional law (such as the EEA/UK), you can manage your ad consent preferences anytime via the Privacy → Ad Choices option inside the app.

3. Sharing Feature

When you tap the Share button, the app generates a score card image on your device and opens your phone’s native share sheet so you can send it to an app of your choice. No contact lists or personal messages are accessed by No Cap Escape.

4. Children’s Privacy

No Cap Escape is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
